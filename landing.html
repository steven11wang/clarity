Why the SAT?

If your goal is a top-30 university in the US, you need a score around 1550. That is the number that decides whether the rest of your application is read at all.

Features

Four · 01–04

01 · Paced path

Learn at the right pace

A guided route through every domain and skill, so you always know what to work on next instead of guessing which weakness to chase.

Path · Domain · Skill

02 · One place

Everything in one place

Lessons, domain practice, full exams, the guide and your words. One place, not six tabs.

Lessons · Exams · Guide

03 · Dictionary

Words, understood fast

Look a word up inside the passage you are reading. The digital SAT tests words in context, so that is where you learn them, and that is how they come back.

Context · Meaning · Word

04 · Return loop

What you miss returns

Missed questions and words come back on a schedule built around when people forget, so nothing quietly rots in an error log you never open.

Attempt · Reflect · Return
