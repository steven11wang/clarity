## Overview

A living Chinese ink-wash landscape on warm cream paper. The source painting remains the visual truth; motion should feel as if moisture, air, and pigment inside the paper have begun to move, never as if modern effects were laid over it.

## Composition

- Focal path: distant river bend to the lower pool.
- Secondary anchor: the pavilion settles on the right bank in the final framing.
- Background: untouched cream-paper sky and distant washed ridges.
- Midground: successive ridge bands revealed through drifting mist.
- Foreground: dark pine cliffs and the widening river pool.

## Motion

- One unbroken virtual-camera descent with three gentle positional phases and a final hold.
- Mist moves laterally and vertically at different rates, with slight opacity changes.
- Ripples remain understated and flatten during the final half-second.
- Three ridge-edge ink blooms appear briefly as the camera brings those planes forward.
- A single soft-focus dip occurs inside the last mist band, then resolves fully before the camera rests.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Preserve monochrome sepia ink, paper grain, and negative space.
- Keep all motion slow, organic, and bounded.
- Do not introduce text, logos, people, modern objects, saturated color, hard cuts, or decorative UI.
