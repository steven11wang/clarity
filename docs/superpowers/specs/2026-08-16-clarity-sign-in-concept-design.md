# Clarity Sign-In Concept: The Quiet Archive

## Objective

Create a high-fidelity desktop concept image for Clarity's sign-in page. The page should feel calm, premium, scholarly, and trustworthy while remaining recognizably part of Clarity's existing visual identity.

## Visual Direction

The concept uses a restrained editorial composition inspired by a beautifully printed academic journal. Warm ivory paper, generous whitespace, charcoal typography, muted jade details, and one cinnabar-red seal create a quiet, confident atmosphere. A faint monochrome shanshui landscape adds depth without competing with the sign-in task.

The concept deliberately removes the existing page's neon effects, floating fusion orb, dense console labels, and overtly futuristic imagery. Digital character comes through precision, spacing, and subtle interface details rather than spectacle.

## Composition

- Desktop canvas with a 16:10 presentation ratio.
- Two-zone layout with approximately 44% devoted to the sign-in experience and 56% to atmospheric artwork.
- The left zone is a warm paper surface with the Clarity wordmark at the top and the authentication form vertically centered.
- The right zone contains a low-contrast ink-wash mountain landscape fading naturally into the paper canvas.
- A narrow vertical rule and gradual tonal transition separate the zones without creating a hard split.

## Brand and Typography

- Display typography: elegant literary serif with restrained contrast.
- Interface typography: clean humanist sans serif for labels, buttons, and supporting text.
- Primary colors: warm ivory, ink charcoal, parchment gray, and muted jade.
- Accent: cinnabar red used only for the small Clarity seal and a minimal focus detail.
- The wordmark reads exactly `Clarity`.

## Sign-In Content

The visible default state is sign-in, not registration or password recovery.

- Heading: `Welcome back`
- Supporting line: `Continue your practice with clarity.`
- Primary social action: `Continue with Google`
- Divider: `or continue with email`
- Email field label: `Email address`
- Password field label: `Password`
- Utility link: `Forgot password?`
- Primary action: `Sign in`
- Secondary action: `New to Clarity? Create an account`
- Quiet footer note: `Your progress stays with you.`

## Interaction Signals

Although the deliverable is a static concept, controls should look implementation-ready:

- Clear form labels remain visible above inputs.
- Inputs use thin charcoal borders, comfortable height, and generous padding.
- The primary button uses dark ink rather than a saturated brand color.
- Focus and selected details use muted jade.
- Error, loading, and recovery states are not shown in the first concept.

## Accessibility and Responsive Intent

- Text and controls maintain strong contrast against the ivory background.
- The form hierarchy remains understandable without relying on color.
- Controls should appear large enough for comfortable pointer and touch use.
- On mobile, the artwork becomes a shallow atmospheric header while the form occupies the main viewport.

## Image-Generation Brief

Generate one polished, high-fidelity UI mockup rather than a mood board or collage. The interface must be front-facing, rectilinear, and realistically implementable. Typography must be legible and limited to the approved copy. Avoid device frames, hands, people, dramatic shadows, glowing neon, glassmorphism, dashboard widgets, decorative charts, excessive Chinese text, watermarks, and extra logos.

## Success Criteria

- The sign-in task is immediately obvious.
- The page feels premium and scholarly within one glance.
- The artwork supports rather than competes with the form.
- The concept is recognizably Clarity through its wordmark, seal, ink landscape, and restrained palette.
- The result could be implemented responsively without changing its core hierarchy.
