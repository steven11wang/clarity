# Clarity Hero Visual — 澄明之境

## Objective

Create a standalone right-side hero illustration for Clarity's landing page. It should inherit the editorial impact of the Hermes Agent hero—one concentrated, engraving-like image balanced against large copy—while replacing Greek imagery with an original Chinese ink-painting visual system.

## Format and Placement

- Portrait artwork at a 4:5 aspect ratio, composed for the right half of a desktop hero.
- No typography inside the artwork.
- The artwork sits on a warm xuan-paper ground that can blend into Clarity's existing `#EDE3CE` page background.
- Visual density is concentrated on the right and lower-right; forms dissolve into mist and negative space toward the left edge so nearby page copy remains legible.
- The important forms stay inside the central 80% safe area for responsive cropping.

## Composition

The scene is an imagined landscape rather than a reproduction of any historical painting. Layered mountains and riverbanks use the long horizontal rhythm, shifting viewpoints, and dry-brush texture associated with *Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains*. A slender river-and-ribbon path travels through the scene in one continuous motion, borrowing the flowing line and narrative cadence of *Nymph of the Luo River* without copying its figures or episodes.

The river-and-ribbon subtly bends into an open loop: a visual metaphor for Clarity's practice, feedback, and return cycle. The loop must remain organic and partly obscured by mist, never becoming a literal infinity symbol.

A low-contrast bronze cloud-and-thunder pattern forms a partial structural halo behind the mountains. It should read as archaeological ornament discovered inside the image, not as a decorative frame pasted on top. A single irregular cinnabar seal shape anchors the lower-right corner; it contains no legible characters.

## Style and Palette

- Medium: refined Chinese ink wash combined with fine engraved linework.
- Ground: warm xuan paper `#EDE3CE` with subtle natural fibre.
- Primary: warm near-black ink `#17120D`, with multiple diluted grey-brown washes.
- Secondary: restrained bronze-patina green `#49746B`, applied only to parts of the river, distant mist, and small mineral accents.
- Accent: one cinnabar mark `#B3382B`.
- Mood: calm, intelligent, spacious, ancient but not nostalgic; premium editorial rather than fantasy concept art.

## Visual References to Translate

- *Nymph of the Luo River*: continuous fine line, drifting ribbon movement, episodic spatial rhythm.
- *Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains*: layered banks, dry-brush ridges, shifting distance, generous breathing space.
- Shang and Zhou bronze ornament: squared cloud-and-thunder geometry and the suggestion of a symmetrical mask structure, used only as supporting order.
- Hermes Agent landing page: concentrated right-side emblematic artwork, strong silhouette, and clear separation between image and copy.

## Constraints

- Do not reproduce a recognizable section, figure, or composition from either historical scroll.
- Do not depict a prominent goddess, scholar, warrior, temple, dragon, phoenix, or ritual vessel.
- Do not use literal education or technology symbols such as books, screens, brains, light bulbs, graduation caps, circuit boards, or robots.
- Do not create a literal yin-yang, infinity sign, logo, badge, mandala, or symmetrical poster.
- Avoid photorealism, anime, game-concept styling, bright blue-green landscape pigments, glossy gold, dense calligraphy, borders, watermarks, or readable text.
- Keep the leftmost quarter quiet and low contrast.

## Deliverable

Generate one high-quality first-pass raster image for review. Save the approved project-bound asset under `public/brand/` with a descriptive versioned filename. Further revisions should change one variable at a time while preserving the approved composition and palette.

## Acceptance Criteria

The image succeeds when it reads first as a distinctive modern Clarity brand image, second as a Chinese ink landscape, and only on closer inspection reveals the bronze structure and learning-loop metaphor. At desktop hero size, the silhouette must remain clear, the left edge must blend naturally into the page, and no unwanted text or modern educational iconography may appear.
